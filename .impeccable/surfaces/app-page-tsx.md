# Surface: rubinowe.pl landing (PL + EN)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: IT companies needing development capacity, and people interested in Rubinowe's own products, weighted equally. Action: email rubinowe@rubinowe.pl. No proof material; content stays general.

Memorable moment: the ruby papercut rosette, cut from code glyphs, unfolding from one folded half.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One sheet, folded once, cut with precision. Rubinowe's two models (services for IT companies, own products) are the two mirrored halves of one papercut. Refuses the category default of a dark hero with gradient, subcopy, button and a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: White paper ground (#FBFBF9), ruby paper (#E30613) with deeper ruby (#9E0A16) for the under-layer, ink (#1A0D10). No midtones and no gradients: hard paper edges with real cast shadows between layers. Ornament is wycinanka geometry (rosettes, mirrored trees, borders), built from code glyphs { } < / > ; and cut precisely, never folk-soft. Grotesque display set heavy and tight, plain workhorse body.

STORY: A visitor sees a precise ruby cut, reads "software house for IT teams and our own products", understands the two models as mirrored halves, sees how we work, and writes an email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav top (logo left; Services, How we work, Contact, PL/EN right). A large ruby rosette fills the right ~55%, unfolding from a folded half on load. The H1 sits bottom-left at about 80px, the email link directly under it as the primary action.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 of 7 (Wycinanka). seed:39c3f574eb790dd5. Raises: one scroll timeline drives every paper layer (automata); no midtones, hard edges (cabaret); state shown by form (cut/folded/flat), not hue (emission rail); folds settle with physical damping (VU meter). Signature interaction: the fold. Each section's papercut unfolds along its mirror axis as it enters, driven by scroll. The contact section is drenched ruby paper.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
