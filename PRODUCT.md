# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (current major), static export, no backend. Bilingual routes `/pl` and `/en`. Replaces the previous Next.js 12 + styled-components + SendGrid site.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:
- IT companies (CTOs, engineering and project managers) looking for extra development capacity or a partner to own a defined part of a project.
- People and companies interested in Rubinowe's own software products (partners, potential users, collaborators).

Both arrive to understand quickly what Rubinowe is and whether to write an email.

## Product Purpose

Rubinowe is a software house. It provides development services to other IT companies (team extension, delivery of well-defined parts of projects) and builds its own digital products. The site exists to present that dual model credibly and lead to one action: an email to rubinowe@rubinowe.pl.

## Positioning

A software house that both serves other technology teams and builds and maintains its own products, so it "lives with the code it writes". Practical, partner-minded, transparent.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact only by email (mailto link to rubinowe@rubinowe.pl). No contact form, no backend.
- Two languages: Polish and English, full parity.
- Content stays general: no figures, client names, case studies, project lists, or team photos.
- Must stay fast and light, including on mobile.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Rubinowe ("ruby-coloured" in Polish). Legal name in footer: Rubinowe Sp. z o.o.
- Logo is kept: `public/logo.svg`, a ruby-red (#E30613) gem shape with a white "R" and a black wordmark.
- Nothing else from the previous site's look is kept.

## Evidence on Hand

No proof material: no statistics, clients, testimonials, case studies, or product names. Do not fabricate any. The earlier site's claims ("95% of clients", "up to 70% savings") were removed on purpose.

## Product Principles

1. Say what we do plainly. Two models, one email.
2. Promise only behavior (how we work), never results we cannot show.
3. Partner tone: honest, calm, competent, not salesy.
4. Respect the visitor's time and device: fast, readable, bilingual.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard was set. Work to WCAG 2.2 AA and respect reduced-motion preferences.
